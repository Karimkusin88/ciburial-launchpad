Protocol flow

How it works

  1. 01

    Configure

    A reusable launch Config is registered once — fee split, recipients, sale supply and graduation threshold — and can back many token launches.

  2. 02

    Issue

    A new token is issued through the launch factory against that Config, with its own bonding curve (ALC) created in the same transaction.

  3. 03

    Trade

    Anyone can buy and sell along the curve. Reserves and price are held entirely by the curve contract; a 1% fee accrues to the fee vault.

  4. 04

    Graduate

    When the curve reserve reaches the 0.05 WETH threshold, the launch graduates: liquidity migrates to the ARL pool for open-market trading.

  5. 05

    Claim fees

    Creator and partner fee shares are claimed from the fee vault, and LP fees from the graduated pool — all by direct contract calls.

Powered by @ammora-protocol/sdk 0.3.9. The launchpad talks to Ammora contracts directly through the chain RPC — no intermediary service holds funds or keys.
